Etsy photo sizes, in plain terms

  • 2000px on the longest side is Etsy’s recommended listing-photo size, so buyers can zoom in on the detail.
  • Under 1MB keeps your listing fast to load. JPEG at 80–85% quality is usually enough for a sharp photo.
  • Use real photos of the actual item. Etsy requires at least one photo that shows the real item as it is — this tool only resizes, crops, and compresses a real photo; it never creates or alters what is in the picture.

This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by Etsy. Sizes and requirements can change; check Etsy’s help pages for the current rules before you publish a listing.

Privacy

There is no account, no sign-up, and no upload. Your photo is read and resized by code running in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing is stored. Closing the page removes it.
